IDX30 Stock Portfolio Optimization with Machine Learning & Risk Parity

Undergraduate Thesis · 2026

Forecasting the daily returns of 21 IDX30 stocks using Bi-LSTM, then allocating optimal portfolio weights with Black-Litterman and Hierarchical Risk Parity.

+ Click for method & results

Method

A Bidirectional LSTM model forecasts daily returns; the predictions feed into a Black-Litterman framework to generate adaptive posterior expected returns, and portfolio weights are then optimized using the Hierarchical Risk Parity (HRP) algorithm.

Results

  • Average prediction Mean Squared Error (MSE): 0.0017
  • Out-of-sample Cumulative Return: 13.18%
  • Sharpe Ratio 0.8966, Calmar Ratio 2.7414
  • Outperformed the traditional historical covariance approach

Risk & Actuarial

Survival Analysis of Sidoarjo Divorce Data (Cox Proportional Hazard)

Research Report · 2024

Modeling the risk and survival probability of marriages using religious court data from Sidoarjo (2019–2023, 120 cases) to understand the drivers of divorce.

+ Click for method & results

Method

Court data preprocessing, survival curve estimation with Kaplan-Meier and Log-Rank tests across groups, and Parametric Survival modeling (Weibull distribution) alongside Cox Proportional Hazard. When the proportional hazard assumption was violated, it was addressed with Time-Dependent Covariates and a Stratified Cox Model.

Results

  • Number of children was shown to significantly affect divorce risk (Log-Rank test, p < 0.005)
  • Couples without children showed the fastest decline in marriage "survival" probability
  • Type of divorce filing and occupation also shaped distinct risk patterns across groups

Financial Statement Analysis & Stock Forecasting of Bank Syariah Indonesia (BSI)

Case Study · 2024

A comprehensive fundamental, technical, governance, risk, and ESG analysis to assess the long-term investment case for BSI stock.

+ Click for method & results

Method

Financial ratio analysis (profitability, liquidity, solvency, activity, market ratios), technical analysis using MACD & RSI, stock price forecasting with ARIMA(1,1,1) and Prophet (validated with Ljung-Box & White Noise tests), and an evaluation of Good Corporate Governance and the ESG framework.

Results

  • Strong fundamentals with improving profitability
  • ARIMA & Prophet models projected a stable upward price trend through 2026
  • Governance and ESG practices support BSI as a long-term investment choice
