Survival Analysis of Sidoarjo Divorce Data (Cox Proportional Hazard)

Group Research Report, 5 members · 2024

Models the risk and survival probability of marriages using religious court data from Sidoarjo (2019 to 2023, 120 cases) to understand what drives divorce.

+Click for method and results

Method

Court data preprocessing, survival curve estimation with Kaplan-Meier and Log-Rank tests across groups, and Parametric Survival modeling (Weibull distribution) alongside Cox Proportional Hazard. When the proportional hazard assumption was violated, it was corrected with Time-Dependent Covariates and a Stratified Cox Model.

120 Court Cases→ Kaplan-Meier and Log-Rank→ Cox PH→ Stratified / Time-Dependent Cox

ARIMA Forecasting of Daily Temperature in Semarang

Group Project, 3 members · 2024

Forecasts Semarang's average daily temperature from 2020 to 2023 using ARIMA, to support climate adaptation and city planning.

+Click for method and results

Method

Box-Cox transformation and a KPSS stationarity test on 1,355 daily climate records, followed by differencing, ARIMA candidate selection using ACF/PACF, and diagnostic checks including the Kolmogorov-Smirnov normality test and the Ljung-Box white noise test.

Box-Cox + KPSS Test→ Differencing→ ARIMA Model Selection→ Forecast

Multiple Linear Regression on Laptop Pricing

Group Project, 3 members · 2024

Analyzes how brand and technical specifications affect laptop prices for Apple, Asus, and Samsung.

+Click for method and results

Method

Multiple linear regression estimated first with no elimination, then refined with backward elimination, followed by residual assumption checks: Kolmogorov-Smirnov normality, Durbin-Watson autocorrelation, variance inflation factor for multicollinearity, and Breusch-Pagan heteroskedasticity.

Full Model (None Elimination)→ Backward Elimination→ Residual Assumption Tests→ Best Model

Results

Adjusted R², Full Model vs Backward Elimination

Final model: Price = 341.421 minus 407.390 for Asus (versus Apple) plus 131.012 per unit increase in RAM.

  • Backward elimination raised the adjusted R² from 68.76% to 81.49%, using just two predictors: brand (Asus) and RAM
  • Passed every residual assumption check: normality, no autocorrelation, no multicollinearity, no heteroskedasticity
  • RAM was the strongest driver of price, consistent with buyer intuition about performance specs
